Daycare & Boarding

Outdoor play by day, cozy lodging by night

Your dog will enjoy our 18 outdoor yards, the company of round-the-clock staff, and a routine customized to their temperament, size and energy.

The yards

Room to run on paw-friendly turf

Our spacious pet-turf yards provide a healthy, fun, and clean outdoor play environment that's easy on the paws, with no rocks, mulch, or mud. The turf is recyclable and water-saving, part of what makes us a certified Green Business.

  • 18 outdoor yards, including two spacious main yards
  • Paw-friendly recyclable turf with no rocks, mulch, or mud
  • Shade, fresh water, and cozy dens in every rotation
  • Water-saving design, part of our Green Business certification
Boarding

A vacation of their own

Boarding pups spend the day outside in the same play groups as our daycare dogs, then retire to their own comfy quarters with heat and A/C, plush beds and blankets, and calm spa music.

Bring something familiar from home if you like (a bed, some bones) or use ours. Meals run on a steady schedule (breakfast ~6am, lunch ~12pm, dinner ~4pm), and we can accommodate timed medications as well as supplements and food additives. Just label everything with your dog's name and clear directions. Lamb-and-rice kibble is available for a fee if you don't bring food.

Want photos of your pup? Email your name and your dog's name to pupdates@alphadoglodging.com for pupdates.

How groups work

Play groups tailored to behavior and temperament

Our staff get to know each dog through a thorough assessment, then place them in a play group based on energy level, temperament, and play style. Groups rotate hourly between play and rest throughout the day, including time in our two main yards, so every dog gets a healthy mix of both.

Easygoing

A calm, easygoing crew for mellow dogs of all sizes who prefer good company over roughhousing.

High Energy

For adolescents and adults who play hard: big yards, well-matched playmates, and plenty of supervised action.

Puppy Groups

Various puppy play groups matched by play style, so young dogs build good social habits with the right peers.

Seniors

A dedicated group for older dogs: gentle company, soft turf, and a pace that suits them.

Private Areas

For special behavioral cases, we offer private yard time with one-on-one staff attention.

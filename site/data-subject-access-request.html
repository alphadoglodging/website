Data Subject Access Request

Last updated September 30, 2026

You can ask what personal information AlphaDog Lodging holds about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or make another request under the privacy laws that apply to you. Our Privacy Policy explains these rights in full.

How to Make a Request

Email your request to julien@alphadoglodging.com with the subject line "Data Subject Access Request". Please include:

  • Your full name.
  • The email address you use with AlphaDog Lodging.
  • Whether you are the person the request is about (or their parent or guardian), or an agent authorized to make the request on their behalf.
  • The law you are making the request under, if you know it: for example the CCPA or the GDPR.
  • What you would like us to do:
    • Tell you what information we collect about you
    • Confirm that your personal information is being processed
    • Give you access to your personal information
    • Correct inaccurate personal information
    • Delete your personal information
    • Opt out of, or opt in to, the sale of your personal data
    • Something else (please describe it)

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

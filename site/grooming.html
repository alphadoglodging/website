Spa FAQ

Good questions about the spa

Does my dog wait in a crate during grooming?
No. Because the spa lives inside AlphaDog, grooming dogs get play time and rest breaks with the pack instead of waiting in a crate, so nervous first-timers tend to settle in faster.
What's included in every package?
Every package includes a nail trim, ear cleaning and plucking (not flushing), anal gland expression, and a blow out. The bath, shampoo, and finishing touches are what set the four packages apart.
Why are some prices shown as a range?
Final pricing depends on your dog's size, coat condition, and temperament, so the bath packages are shown as a range. We'll always confirm the price with you before we start.
Can I add grooming to a daycare or boarding stay?
Yes. Grooming is an easy add-on to any daycare or boarding reservation, and it can also be booked on its own. À la carte add-ons are available too, just ask at the front desk or when you book.
What are the spa's hours?
The spa is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Hours can change, so the more notice we have when booking, the better we can fit your pup in.
How do I book a spa day?
Call us at (415) 383-2111 or email dogs@alphadoglodging.com, and we'll take it from there.

The program

Enrichment built specifically for your dog

Dogs have different interests, whether that's romping on the beach, sniffing on the trail, or playing tug and toss in the yard. We offer on-site and off-site activities daily, added right onto your dog's daycare or boarding visit.

Activities are matched to your dog's energy, skills, and social style. Each session comes with a report card: a summary of the outing with photos or videos from the handler, so you can keep up with the adventure. Siblings who go out together on off-site adventures get a discounted rate.

To get started: email our Outdoors team at outdoors@alphadoglodging.com and note the services you're interested in for any upcoming reservations. We'll send a one-time intake questionnaire to get to know your pup, then set up a schedule.

Hours & scheduling

How the day runs

1

Drop off

Off-site walks and hikes: drop off by 11am (grace period to 11:30am). On-site Tug & Toss, Flirt Pole, Snuggle Session, and Find It!: drop off by 12pm.

2

Adventure

Your dog heads out with the Outdoors team, or gets their structured play session in the yard, worked into their regular daycare, training, or boarding day.

3

Pick up

Pick-up for Outdoors dogs begins at 4pm, so there's plenty of time to finish the day's services. Want a post-adventure bath? Give the Spa team notice and an estimated pickup time.

Outdoors services run daily and attach to any daycare or boarding reservation. Next-day requests by 8pm; same-day requests need approval from the Outdoors team. Cancellations are accepted without penalty until 7am the day of. Standing reservations are welcome but don't apply during holidays and peak periods.

Outdoors FAQ

Good questions before the trail

Can my dog go off-leash?
Off-leash is for Hikes only, and dogs need to prove solid recall first. Pups who've trained with us usually go off-leash easily (our handlers still test their responses); dogs who haven't will start on-leash while our walkers assess their skills. As much as we want them to run free, everyone's safety comes first.
Do I bring my own equipment?
We prefer harnesses (chest-leading or vest-style) for safety, since they're the least likely to tangle or cause issues during play. Bring your own if you like, or use ours; we have plenty of sizes and styles. We always use our own leashes, so no need to provide those.
Do they get treats?
Many walkers carry treats as rewards for good behavior, and to encourage a good photo op. We use high-quality freeze-dried beef liver; if your pup has sensitivities, allergies, or is just picky, please provide kibble or treats especially for these outings.
Are there post-outing baths?
Our Spa team would love to have your pooch pop in for a spell. We just need enough time after their adventure. Share an estimated pickup time when scheduling and we'll coordinate with the grooming team. The more notice, the better!
Can we book multiple services?
Absolutely. Many owners do an on-site play session in the morning and a hike in the afternoon. The daily roster depends on staff availability, but we can generally accommodate multiple services, even across programs. Just ask when scheduling.
How do I book?
Email outdoors@alphadoglodging.com with the services you're interested in and any upcoming reservations you'd like them attached to. We'll send a one-time intake questionnaire about your pup's off-site behaviors, then set up a schedule.
What happens if I'm late?
Drop-off for walks and hikes is by 11am, with a grace period until 11:30am, after which a late fee applies. On-site sessions have a noon drop-off. Because outings head out as a small group at a set time, please call ahead at (415) 383-2111 if you're running behind so we can plan around it.

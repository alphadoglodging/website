Training

Training that lasts a lifetime

AlphaDog University is our training program, designed and run day to day by Mike Wombacher, one of the Bay Area's most experienced trainers.

Training

Two ways to train with us

We follow the LIMA principle (Least Intrusive, Minimally Aversive), starting with positive reinforcement and reward-based techniques, building trust between dog and handler. Our trainers always ask for consent before introducing a new tool.

Daycare & Train or Board & Train

How it works

Training is an add-on to a daycare day or boarding stay:

Daycare & Train

Train by day, home at night

Your dog trains during the day and goes home with you in the evening. We recommend starting with 2–3 days a week. Ideal for obedience foundations, social skills in group settings, and reinforcing existing training, all while your dog's needs are met during your workday.

Daycare rate: $61/day + training fee

Board & Train

The immersive option

Your dog stays with us and receives structured daily training. We recommend starting with a two-week board & train. Ideal for jump-starting obedience, tackling more complex behavioral challenges, and dogs who thrive on a highly structured environment.

Boarding rate: $90 per 24 hours + training fee

Why train here

Trained where they already play

Most training happens in a quiet room, then falls apart the moment real life shows up. At AlphaDog, training is built into your dog's daycare or boarding day, so they practice new skills surrounded by other dogs, movement, and everyday distractions, the exact conditions where you actually need those skills to hold.

What they learn here is what comes home with you: focus that survives a busy sidewalk, manners that hold around other dogs, and a calmer, more confident dog in the places you go together.

About Mike

The trainer behind the program

Mike is the author of three books: There's a Puppy in the House, Good Dog, Happy Baby, and Integrated Dog Training: The Commonsense Visual Guide to Training Any Dog. He has been certified as an expert in dog behavior by the California Superior Court, has been a guest speaker and panelist at the annual California Animal Care and Control Convention, and has appeared on radio and television programs nationwide. He has also written columns and articles about dog behavior for Dog Fancy, Dog World, American Dog, Fit Pregnancy, Boston Whaler, and smaller publications.

Over the years, Mike has trained dogs for high-profile clients in the entertainment, financial, and academic worlds, including Charles Schwab, Robin Williams, Linda Ronstadt, Barry Levinson, Chad Hurley (founder of YouTube), Max Levchin (co-founder of PayPal), Jeremy Stoppelman (founder of Yelp), Dr. Robert Sapolsky (author of many books on animal behavior and neurobiology), Joe Satriani, Bob Weir of the Grateful Dead, Kirk Hammett of Metallica, and Michael Tilson Thomas, music director of the San Francisco Symphony:

Scheduling & FAQ

Hours, policies, and good questions

What is your training approach?
We train in real life, not in a quiet room. Sessions are built into your dog's daycare or boarding day, so they practice new skills around other dogs, movement, and everyday distractions, the same conditions where those skills need to hold up at home and around town. Our program is led by Mike Wombacher, and the focus is always on practical results: reliable focus, good manners around other dogs, and a calmer, more confident dog. When training wraps up, a complimentary tutorial shows you exactly how to keep it going.
When does training run?
Training is available Monday–Friday, booked as a full daycare day (7am–7pm). Drop-off is required by 9am (grace period until 9:30am, after which a late fee applies), and dogs arriving after 10am may not receive training. Because sessions are woven into the rhythm of the daycare day, there may also be especially busy days when we can't fit a session in. Pick-up begins at 2:30pm, but dogs are welcome to stay until 7pm at no extra charge.
Can I join the training sessions?
For liability reasons, clients can't join sessions, but we have two ways to stay involved. Every dog gets one complimentary tutorial, where a trainer walks you through hand signals, verbal commands, and any new tools. For more hands-on work, private training ($195/hour) brings a trainer to your home or another agreed location.
Why would my dog need private training?
Some challenges show up in places we can't recreate here: pulling on the leash in your neighborhood, jumping on guests at the front door, or reactivity on your usual walking route. Private training ($195/hour) brings a trainer to your home or another agreed location to work on those specific situations with you. It's also the best way to learn the handling yourself, so the whole household stays consistent and the progress lasts.
What treats do you use?
High-quality freeze-dried beef liver, with freeze-dried chicken breast, salmon, and string cheese as alternates. For shy dogs we usually recommend skipping breakfast to boost motivation. For picky eaters, allergies, puppies under 1 year, or sensitive stomachs, please provide your own training treats. And for any dog, you're always welcome to send your favorite treats from home. We're happy to use them.
What are the late, no-show, and cancellation policies?
Late arrivals: first is a reminder, second is a $25 fee, third is a $61 fee. Please call ahead if you're running behind. No-shows: reminder first, then half the training + daycare/boarding fee, then the full fee. Cancellations are free until 7am the day of; after that, a $25 cancellation fee applies.
How do I book?
All daycare, boarding, and training reservations go through the front desk. Email dogs@alphadoglodging.com, call (415) 383-2111, or stop by. Next-day reservations by 7pm; same-day bookings need approval from the training team. Standing reservations are welcome, but don't apply during holidays and peak periods.
Is service dog training part of this program?
Yes! AlphaDog Assist, our service dog program, is led by Sierra Berrick, our service dog training director and mental health specialist, alongside Mike Wombacher. We train psychiatric service, medical alert, migraine alert, allergen alert, and mobility assistance dogs.
What happens if I'm late?
Drop-off is required by 9am, with a grace period until 9:30am. After that a late fee applies: the first time is just a reminder, the second is $25, and the third is $61. Dogs arriving after 10am may not be able to train that day, but they're still welcome to stay for daycare. If you're running behind, please call ahead at (415) 383-2111.
